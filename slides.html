AI's self-improvement?

Claude Fable 5

Launched June 9 - then disabled June 12

Reception

  • Consistently positive from developers
  • Across the board stronger than previous models - and seemingly stronger than other companies' too
  • Top spot on both arena.ai leaderboards: agents and webdev code

Limitations

  • Cybersecurity and biology/chemistry questions fall back to Opus 4.8
  • On AI-training questions, the model is designed to perform worse - no visible fallback
  • The goal: keep competitors from using it to build their own models
  • system card, p. 12

The twist: it's now disabled

June 12 - US export control directive
  • Directive bars foreign nationals (inside and outside the US) from Fable 5 and Mythos 5; Anthropic disabled both models for all customers rather than selectively block
  • Amazon found a jailbreak surfacing info useful for cyberattacks; its CEO raised it with senior officials, and Commerce Secretary Lutnick sent the order to Amodei
  • The government's position: the order came after Amodei refused to patch or de-deploy. Senior Anthropic staff went to Washington DC to meet White House officials
  • CNBC · Axios · NBC News

The dispute

  • Anthropic: minor, previously-known vulnerabilities also present in other public models (names OpenAI's GPT-5.5); a narrow jailbreak shouldn't pull a model used by hundreds of millions statement
  • But neither side has published the technique or the specific flaws - the "other models too" claim isn't a demonstrated head-to-head
  • Independent expert Katie Moussouris (CEO, Luta Security) says she's seen the report: "it's not a jailbreak" - defensive prompting that found known vulnerabilities - and calls it "a complete overreaction" / "an own goal" her post
